Research Overview

My undergraduate work has introduced me to catalyst testing, adsorption experiments, and the care needed to make an experimental result meaningful.

How I approach experimental work

In Dr. Praveen Bollini’s research group, I’ve worked with fixed-bed reactors and used analytical tools to connect a material’s behavior to its structure and operating conditions. My experience began with Ga/ZSM-5 catalysts for sustainable fuel applications and continued into CO₂ adsorption and mixed-metal sorbents.

The most useful part of that work has been learning to look at a result in context. Before comparing two materials or two runs, I need to understand how they were prepared, what conditions they experienced, and how the data were processed. That is the approach I want to keep developing as I take on more responsibility in research.
