Catalyst testing · Summer 2025

Catalysis and Sustainable Fuels

My Ga/ZSM-5 work introduced me to the connection between catalyst properties, reaction conditions, and product distribution.

From reactor testing to interpretation

During my summer research experience, I conducted fixed-bed tests with ethanol and ethylene feeds over Ga/ZSM-5 catalysts for sustainable jet fuel applications. I used reactor measurements alongside XRD and BET characterization to investigate catalyst behavior.

This was an early opportunity to see that the same material can be described in several ways. A reactor test tells me about its performance under a particular set of conditions, while characterization tells me something about its phase, surface area, or porosity. Learning to connect those measurements helped me move beyond looking at each result separately.

How the methods contribute to the research
Method What it helps me evaluate
Fixed-bed testing Catalyst performance under controlled feed and operating conditions
Gas chromatography Product distribution and aromatic selectivity
X-ray diffraction Crystalline phases, including gallium oxide species
BET analysis Surface area and porosity

Why this experience matters to my next steps

This work helped establish my interest in catalysis and materials. I want to become more confident explaining why a catalyst behaves the way it does and designing comparisons that can distinguish between possible explanations. My adsorption work has given me additional practice with experimental consistency and data interpretation, which I can bring back to those questions.
