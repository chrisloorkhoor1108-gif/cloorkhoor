Research interests and next steps

Research Interests and Goals

My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD in chemical engineering and develop the depth to work on research questions that I find meaningful.

A developing direction

I’m especially interested in catalysis and materials, including how a material’s structure and operating conditions affect what it can do. CO₂ capture has given me practical research experience, but I want to explore a broader range of catalytic processes and industrial applications before narrowing my focus.

I enjoy research because there is room to ask why a result occurred and what I should test next. A PhD appeals to me as an opportunity to develop that ability further and take more ownership of a research problem. I’m still exploring the specific career path I want afterward, but I know I want work that keeps me engaged with difficult technical questions.

What I’m working toward now

For now, I want to follow through on the Ni–Ce experiment plan, strengthen my experimental reasoning, and continue building experience with computational and process models. I’m also seeking engineering internship experience for summer 2027 so I can better understand how these skills are used outside the university.

I want my next decisions to be based on work I’ve actually done and questions I want to keep pursuing. As that experience grows, I’ll update this portfolio to show how my interests and preparation are developing.
